## Guidelines for new images
- Background: pure white or very light grey.
- Product images: 4:3 landscape, at least 1600 × 1200 px.
- Hero image: square 1:1, at least 1200 × 1200 px.
- PNG or high-quality JPG; keep each under ~1 MB for fast page loading (hero is currently 1.9 MB — compress before going live).
