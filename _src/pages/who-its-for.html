Where the money goes with free-pour liquids

A 5-litre can sits in the store room and staff pour by eye. Some days it's double the dose, some days it's half. You pay for the overpour, and you can't tell how far a can should go. Liquid cleaners are also mostly water, so you pay to transport and store it.

What a sachet changes

  • Same dose every time. One sachet, one bottle, one litre. No measuring cups, no training beyond a single dosing card.
  • You can count it. Sachets used per week tells you exactly what housekeeping or the kitchen consumes.
  • Less to store. Five sachets make the same 5 litres as a 5-litre can of liquid.
